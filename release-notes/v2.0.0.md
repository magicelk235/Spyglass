Spyglass 2.0 previews almost anything you press Space on in Finder, not just Google Drive files.

### Added
- Quick Look previews for Markdown, source code, JSON, XML, plist, YAML, TOML, diffs, Jupyter notebooks, TSV, JSON Lines, SQLite databases, archives (zip, tar, gzip, bzip2, xz, ISO), Java, Android, Python, NuGet, VS Code and Firefox packages, apps, installers, disk images, frameworks, binaries, folders, torrents and signed configuration profiles.
- Previews refresh when the file is saved.
- Settings window: your plan, Google Drive sign-in, theme, code font size, and a switch for each format.
- Finder thumbnails for Google documents on the Drive plan, showing the document's first page.
- Settings shows how many previews you used this week, by format.
- Automatic updates.
- A 7-day free trial of every format preview, with no card.
- New plans: Spyglass Pro ($12 once) and Spyglass Drive ($4 a month).

### Changed
- Rendered Google Drive previews are part of Spyglass Drive. If you bought Spyglass 1.0, your key keeps working and includes Spyglass Drive at no charge.
- After the trial, files without a plan open as plain text or a file summary instead of the rich preview. Google Drive info cards stay free.
- Full Disk Access is only requested on the Drive plan.

### Fixed
- Paid Drive previews never appeared on macOS 15 and later. Your license and cached previews move over automatically the first time 2.0 opens.
